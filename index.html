Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Email support@subhanallahstudios.com and we'll get back to you.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open the Shop and tap Restore. On Android, purchases come back automatically the next time the game opens.

How do I redeem a code?

Open the Shop and tap Redeem Code. On iPhone the App Store's code sheet opens over the game; on Android the Play Store's redeem page opens. Your reward arrives when you return to the game.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where privacy rules apply (for example the EU, UK and some US states), open Settings and tap Privacy Choices.

The free coins video won't play

Videos need an internet connection and can take a moment to load. Wait a minute and try again.
